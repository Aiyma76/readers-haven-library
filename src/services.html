WHAT WE OFFER

Our Digital Services

We provide modern facilities to make reading, studying, and accessing information easier.

🔎

Online Catalogue

Search for books, authors, and available collections through our online catalogue.

How to Use:

  1. Open the library catalogue.
  2. Enter a book title or author.
  3. Check the availability of the book.
Available 24/7
📚

E-Books

Read digital books and educational materials using your computer, tablet, or smartphone.

How to Use:

  1. Log in with your library membership.
  2. Browse the digital collection.
  3. Select and read an available e-book.
Available 24/7
🪑

Study Room Booking

Reserve a quiet study room for individual learning, research, or group discussions.

How to Use:

  1. Visit the library booking desk.
  2. Select an available study room.
  3. Confirm your preferred time slot.
During library hours
🖨️

Printing & Scanning

Print assignments, documents, and educational materials or scan important papers.

How to Use:

  1. Visit the printing service desk.
  2. Submit your document.
  3. Collect your printed or scanned copy.
During library hours
📶

Free Wi-Fi

Enjoy internet access for online research, educational resources, and academic work.

How to Use:

  1. Enable Wi-Fi on your device.
  2. Select the library Wi-Fi network.
  3. Follow the connection instructions.
During library hours
