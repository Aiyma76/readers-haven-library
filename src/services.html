WHAT WE OFFER

Our Library Services

Explore the facilities available at Readers' Haven.

🔎

Online Catalogue

Search books by title, author or category. Check availability anytime through our online catalogue.

Timing: Available 24/7.

📱

E-Books

Members can access digital books using their library account and read them online.

Timing: Available 24/7.

📚

Study Room Booking

Reserve a study room by contacting the library desk. Rooms are available on a first-come basis.

Timing: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–8 PM.

🖨️

Printing and Scanning

Members can print documents and scan important study materials by requesting assistance at the service desk.

Timing: Monday–Saturday, during library hours.

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Visitors can use free Wi-Fi for research, online learning and educational activities. Ask staff for login details.

Timing: During library opening hours.
